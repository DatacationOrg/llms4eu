Rows are the domain problems from search task v3; columns are the three goals.

**The main gaps:**

1. **Techniques against documentation skew *inside retrieval*.** The problem and
   its measurement are well covered; the techniques found all rerank whole
   recommendations, not retrieved passages.
2. **Retrieval-side language debiasing.** The one technique read (DKM-RAG) works
   at generation; Amiraz's quota is the only retrieval fix so far.
3. **Location × language.** Nothing in the literature combines them; only H1–H5.
4. **Scale/speed.** Untouched. Fine while storage is out of scope, but speed is
   one of the team's two stated goals.

## 3. What the proxy data can and cannot support until December

Facts from `decisions.md` and the eval code `[docs]`; implications `[claude]`.

**Can support:**

- **Cross-border experiments.** 10 localities in 8 countries, including a
  Croatian cluster 35 km from Brestanica and two Czech clusters 8 km apart.
- **Multilingual experiments.** 8 corpus languages; a `crosslingual` question
  type already exists.
- **Missing-metadata experiments.** 690 of 1,338 pages are located, so roughly
  half are not.
- **Measuring documentation skew** in the corpus (pages and chunks per place),
  and place-level metrics, since located pages carry a Wikidata ID.
- **Testing evaluation instruments** end to end, before the real data arrives.

**Cannot support:**

- **Recommendation-style questions.** Every question is factual, generated from
  one chunk, with one gold chunk.
- **A real popularity contrast.** All localities are small; the corpus may lack
  genuinely famous places to contrast hidden gems with. To check.
- **Labels beyond Brestanica.** Approved labels cover only the 176 original pages.
- **Human judgments.** No question has been reviewed by a person.
- **Speed at scale.** 1,338 pages say little about 300K documents.
- **Real user language.** Generated questions reuse the gold chunk's wording.
