# events

* [Gradovi Posavja](gradovi-posavja.md) - A sustainable regional project and informal association established in 2014 that unites the managers of six castles in the Posavje region of Slovenia to enhance their cultural and tourist promotion.
* [Rajhenburški dan čokolade](rajhenburski-dan-cokolade.md) - An annual event at Rajhenburg Castle celebrating the chocolate and liqueur-making heritage of the Trappist monks who were the first industrial chocolate producers in Slovenia.
* [Sejem](sejem.md) - Sejem je ponavljajoča se gospodarska prireditev za razstavljanje in prodajo blaga ali storitev, ki izvira iz srednjeveških trgov ob cerkvenih praznikih in danes služi predvsem informiranju ter primerjavi ponudb.
