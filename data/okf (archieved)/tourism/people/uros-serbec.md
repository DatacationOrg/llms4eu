**Uroš Šerbec** (born 15 February 1968, Celje) is a Slovenian handball player and sports expert commentator.

## Playing Career

### Club

- **RK Krško** (1980–1989)
- **RK Gorenje Velenje** (1989–1991)
- **Celje Pivovarna Laško** (1991–2001)
- **RK Slovan Ljubljana** (2002–2003)
- **RK Dol pri Hrastniku** (2003–2005)
- **RK Krško** (2005–2008)

### National Team

Šerbec played 120 matches for the Slovenian national team and scored 476 goals. He participated in the 1995 World Championship in Iceland and the 2000 Olympic Games in Sydney, Australia, where he was the captain of the team that finished in eighth place.

## Coaching Career

- **RK Maribor** (coach)
- **RK Krško** (coach from 2010): Led the club to the first league in his first year. In the first season under his leadership, the club qualified for the league for the champion of Slovenia.
- **RK Drava Ptuj** (coach, current)

## Media Work

He works as an expert commentator on national TV during handball match broadcasts.

## Personal Life

He is originally from Brestanica.
