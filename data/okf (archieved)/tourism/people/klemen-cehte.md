Klemen Cehte (born 10 May 1986 in Brežice) is a Slovenian handball player who plays as a left back.

## Early Life

He grew up in Brestanica, where he started training handball at around 7 years old with RK Krško.

## Club Career

- MRD Dobova
- RK Krško
- RK Trimo Trebnje
- RK Gorenje Velenje
- Al Sadd SC
- Pays d'Aix UCH (from Aix-en-Provence, a city of around 143,000 inhabitants located 32 km north of Marseille in southwestern France)

## International Career

He plays for the Slovenian national team. As of 22 November 2016, he had 41 appearances and 102 goals.

## Personal Life

His younger brother Nejc Cehte is also a Slovenian international handball player and currently a member of RK Gorenje Velenje.
