# geography

* [Bohor](bohor.md) - Bohor je pogorje v vzhodni Sloveniji, ki tvori južno mejo Kozjanskega in skrajni vzhodni rob Posavskega hribovja, njegov najvišji vrh pa je Veliki Javornik (1024 m).
* [Dolina](dolina.md) - A valley is an elongated depression in the Earth's surface, typically formed by fluvial or glacial erosion, and classified by its V-shaped or U-shaped cross-section.
* [Pokrajine v Sloveniji](pokrajine-v-sloveniji.md) - Različni neformalni modeli delitve Slovenije na pokrajinske enote, ki temeljijo na zgodovinskih kronskih deželah in sodobnih geografskih regionalizacijah.
* [Posavsko hribovje](posavsko-hribovje.md) - Posavsko hribovje is a hilly region in central and eastern Slovenia, situated between the Sava River and the Ljubljana–Zagreb motorway, characterized by east–west oriented Miocene folds such as the Kum, Lisca, and Bohor ridges.
* [Sever](sever.md) - Sever je ena od štirih glavnih strani neba, ki leži nasproti juga in je določena s smerjo proti severnemu tečaju.
* [Statistične regije Slovenije](statisticne-regije-slovenije.md) - The 12 statistical regions of Slovenia, established in 1995 and aligned with the EU NUTS 3 classification, serve as the primary territorial units for collecting and presenting national and Eurostat-comparable regional data.
* [Trg (naselje)](trg-naselje.md) - A historical type of settlement in Slovene lands, originating from medieval market rights, that functioned as a market town distinct from a city primarily by its lack of defensive walls.
* [UTC+01:00](utc-plus-1.md) - A time zone offset one hour ahead of Coordinated Universal Time, used as Central European Time and West Africa Time across much of Europe and parts of Africa.
* [UTC+02:00](utc-plus-2.md) - A time zone identifier representing an offset of +02:00 from Coordinated Universal Time, used as standard time in parts of Africa and as summer time in most of Central Europe.
* [Časovni pas](time-zones.md) - Časovni pasovi so deli Zemljine površine, ki imajo določen isti čas, teoretično 24 pasov od UTC−12:00 do UTC+14:00, v praksi pa meje ustrezajo administrativnim enotam ali geografskim posebnostim.
