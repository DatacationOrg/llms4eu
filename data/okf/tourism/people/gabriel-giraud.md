## Slovenski biografski leksikon

Gabriel Giraud (in religion Kamil), Trappist, founder of the monastery in Rajhenburg, born 7 June 1836 in Lyon, died 28 February 1899 in Rajhenburg.

After completing secondary school at the institution of Our Dear Lady of the Minims in Lyon, he remained in his father's silk wholesale business. After his father's death in 1868, his decision to enter the Trappist order matured; he did so in mid-1871, having previously volunteered as an artilleryman in the war with Germany.

In 1880, the French government forcibly dissolved the Trappist Abbey of Dombes, where Giraud lived. Abbot Augustin de Ladouze decided to seek a residence for the monastic community outside France. Choir oblate Gabriel provided the funds for the purchase of the manor in Rajhenburg, where the Trappists settled in mid-1881, and made it possible for the estate to be organized and expanded, and for a modernly equipped chocolate factory and a steam distillery to be built.

The Rajhenburg monastery was elevated to an abbey in 1891. Giraud, in reality the founder of the abbey, performed various humble services in the monastery as a choir oblate until his death.
