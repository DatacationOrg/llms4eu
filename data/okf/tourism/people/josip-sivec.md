## Slovenski biografski leksikon

Josip Sivec, architect, born 18 February 1896 in Ljubljana to carpenter Ivan, lived there. In Ljubljana he attended primary school 1902–10, 2 years of preparatory arts and crafts vocational school, and in 1914 completed the carpentry-turning department at the State Foreman School. In 1915 he was conscripted into the army; 1919–23 employed in Ljubljana as a technical draftsman at the Inspectorate for Southern Railways, 1923–45 construction manager and designer at the construction company of engineer Dukić, during which time he passed the builder’s examination in 1928 and studied at the Academy of Fine Arts in Vienna (master school of Prof. C. Holzmeister, graduated 1932); 1945–9 he was manager and chief designer at Gradis, 1949–51 chief calculator at Slovenija-projekt, 1951–7 retired due to illness; 1959 again chief designer at the Investment Bureau in Trbovlje, 1961 permanently retired.

### Executed projects

- Several residential buildings and villas in Ljubljana (Erjavčeva Street, Pod Rožnikom, etc.), Belgrade, Celje, Trbovlje, Zagreb, on Rab and on Krk 1931–45
- Several residential and office interiors 1932–60
- Residential blocks of engineer Dukić between Titova, Kidričeva, Župančičeva and Gajeva streets in Ljubljana as the first example of larger-scale row construction in Slovenia 1933–4 (included in the international architecture exhibition in Rabat, Morocco in 1951 and in Athens in 1958)
- Project for mechanisation of mining and transport installations in Trbovlje 1937
- Caloric power plants for TPD in Brestanica (Rajhenburg) 1939–41
- Children’s shelter, pharmacy and residential building, Home for Sisters and doctors at the hospital in Trbovlje 1945–6
- Reconstruction of the Cement factory in Trbovlje
- Paper factory in Radeče near Zidani Most
- Factory halls in the Stol factory in Duplica and glue factory in Ljubljana
- Mechanisation of mining and transport installations in the mines Kanižarica, Črna, Soko (Bosnia and Herzegovina) 1957–60
- Clothing factory Sevnica 1959–60

In 1945–6 he compiled the first post-war norms for construction and other works.

### Personal interests

Sivec collected old clocks and had an interesting, fairly extensive collection.
