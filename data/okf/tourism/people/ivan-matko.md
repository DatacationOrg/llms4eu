Ivan Matko (25 July 1885 – 1945) was a Slovene internist and medical researcher.

## Early Life and Education

Ivan Matko was born on 25 July 1885 in Rajhenburg (now [Brestanica](../destinations/brestanica/index.md)). He attended primary school in his hometown. In 1896, he enrolled at the lower gymnasium in Celje. He completed his fifth year of secondary school in Maribor and the sixth through eighth years in Ljubljana, where he passed his final exams (matura) in 1905. He studied medicine in Vienna, graduating in 1911.

## Medical Career

After graduation, Matko worked as a visiting physician (hospitant) in the surgical department of F. Derganc at the general hospital in Ljubljana. Following his military service in 1912, he became a visiting physician at Professor Ortner's clinic for internal medicine in Vienna. In 1914, he was appointed a demonstrator at the medical clinic.

During the First World War (1914–1918), he served as an assistant in Professor Stejskal's department at Garrison Hospital No. 1 in Vienna. In 1916, he was assigned to a hospital for smallpox in Nový Jičín. He later became the chief physician of the internal medicine department at Archduke Rainer's Hospital in Vienna. Until September 1918, he was the chief of the infectious diseases department at the 2nd Garrison Hospital in Vienna, and until the end of the war, he served as the chief of the central hospital for malaria in Hietzing. Concurrently, from 1917 to 1918, he worked at the pathological institute of Professor Paltauf and the chemical institutes of Professors Pauli and Freund.

In January 1919, he became the chief physician of the temporary military hospital in Maribor. On 1 June 1919, he was appointed head of the internal medicine department (primarij) at the general hospital in Maribor and became a member of the First State Health Council.

On 20 August 1920, he became a private docent for the pathology and therapy of internal diseases at the medical faculty of Charles University in Prague. In November 1920, he was appointed the first assistant at Professor Syllaba's clinic. In 1921, he served as the personal physician to President Masaryk and, in the autumn, conducted a practical course on kidney diseases at the clinic. At the end of 1921, he visited the physical institute of Professor Dessauer in Frankfurt am Main before returning to his position as head of the internal medicine department at the general hospital in Maribor.

He furthered his education by attending medical congresses and conferences in Prague (1923), Belgrade (1926), Rome (1928), Oslo (1930), and Nice (1931), and undertook study trips, including one to the Nordic and Baltic countries in 1930.

## Contributions and Research

At the end of 1921, Matko founded the Maribor anti-tuberculosis league. Under his leadership, the league organized an anti-tuberculosis dispensary in 1922 and completed a tuberculosis ward at the Maribor general hospital in 1927. He also participated in the establishment of a holiday colony at Sv. Martin na Pohorju and the construction of a swimming area on Maribor Island.

His research, documented in approximately 240 publications by early 1933, focused on the stomach, digestive tract, heart, blood, malaria, and tuberculosis. His works were published in journals such as *Wiener Medizinische Wochenschrift*, *Deutsche Medizinische Wochenschrift*, *Medizinische Klinik*, *Archiv für Verdauungskrankheiten*, and various Slovene medical and general publications.

Three of his scientific works were each awarded 1000 gold crowns from the Prince Liechtenstein foundation:

- "Über Wechselbeziehungen zwischen Harn und Chinin in der Haemolyse" (*Wiener klinische Wochenschrift*, 1918, no. 3)
- "Chinin und Leukocyten" (*Zeitschrift für klinische Medizin*, Berlin, 1919, vol. 88)
- "Der lymphatische Apparat u. seine Beziehungen zur Vaccination" (*Zeitschrift für experimentelle Pathologie und Therapie*, 1919, vol. 19)

## Selected Publications

- *Osnovna navodila za zdravljenje malarije* (1919)
- *Boj proti jetiki* (1919)
- *Cilji in naloge ambulatorija protitub. lige v Mariboru* (1922)
- *Pouk o jetiki* (1922)
- *Pomen zraka, vode in svetlobe za človeški organizem* (1926)
- *Protituberkulozni dispanzer v socialnohigienski borbi proti jetiki kot ljudski in kužni bolezni* (1930)
- *Katekizem o tuberkulozi* (1931)
- *Nalezljivo izvrženje pri govedi in Bangova bolezen pri človeku* (1931)
- *Preiskava prsja in trebušja I* (1933)
- *Perkusija in avskultacija I* (1933)
- *Problem stigmatizacije v med. luči* (1933)

He was also preparing a neurological-psychiatric and psychoanalytic dictionary with Robida.
