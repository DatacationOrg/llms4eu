**Milan Pertot** (20 May 1884, Trieste – February 1967, Ljubljana) was a Slovene electrical engineer and technical writer.

## Family

- Father: Simon Pertot, physician from Barcola (Barkovlje) near Trieste
- Mother: Frančiška Eber

## Education

- Matura at the German gymnasium in Trieste
- Studied at the Polytechnic in Prague, graduated in 1909

## Career

- First employment in Prague
- From 1913: Director of the tramway on the Matulji–Opatija–Lovran line
- Left the Littoral region in 1923
- Initially worked as operations manager of the Zagorje ob Savi mine
- From 1932: Head of electrical installations at the Trbovlje mine
- Participated in the construction of the Trbovlje and Brestanica thermal power plants
- During the war, expelled to Sisak in 1941
- After the war, employed at the Coal Directorate of the People's Republic of Slovenia (LRS)

## Writing

Pertot established himself as a technical writer in domestic and foreign press. He devoted the majority of his journalistic work to Nikola Tesla, wishing for both domestic and international audiences to recognise the reality of Tesla's discoveries. These efforts resulted in his book:

- *Nikola Tesla – pionir elektrotehniške dobe* (Nikola Tesla – Pioneer of the Electrotechnical Age), Ljubljana, 1962
