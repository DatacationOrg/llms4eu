# people

* [Adam Bohorič](../adam-bohoric.md) - A 16th-century Slovene Protestant linguist and schoolmaster, best known for authoring the first grammar of the Slovene language, 'Arcticae horulae succisivae' (1584).
* [Aleš Mižigoj](../ales-mizigoj.md) - Slovenski gospodarstvenik, rojen leta 1928 na Plešivem, ki je kot dolgoletni glavni direktor vodil podjetje Medex.
* [Alojz Zoratti](../alojz-zoratti.md) - Alojz Zoratti (1874–1960) was a Slovene gilder and sculptor based in Maribor, known as the last representative of the Styrian woodcarving and gilding tradition that originated in the 19th century.
* [Alojzij Remec](../alojzij-remec.md) - Slovene poet, writer, and playwright (1886–1952) who served as mayor of Ptuj and is known for his expressionist drama Magda and the historical peasant story Veliki punt.
* [Andrej Elsbacher](../andrej-elsbacher.md) - Slovenski veletrgovec in narodni delavec (1835–1905), ki je v Laškem zgradil največje trgovsko podjetje in bil soustanovitelj tamkajšnje čitalnice.
* [Angelo Zoratti](../angelo-zoratti.md) - Angelo Zoratti (1839–1913) was an Italian-born gilder, woodcarver, and painter who established a permanent ecclesiastical art workshop in Maribor and created or restored numerous altars across Slovenian Styria.
* [Anton Laharnar](../anton-laharnar.md) - Anton Laharnar (1909–2001) was a Slovene anti-fascist activist (tigrovec) and lawyer from the Tolmin region who later served as a public prosecutor in Slovenia.
* [Anton Zeit](../anton-zeit.md) - An 18th-century Baroque painter active in the Krško region, known for religious works for Capuchin monasteries and parish churches.
* [Arko, Andrej (1947–)](../arko-andrej.md) - Slovenski prevajalec, pesnik, pisatelj in urednik, znan po prevodih svetovnih klasikov in dolgoletnem vodenju kulturnega programa Radia Slovenija.
* [Avgust Emanuel Reuss](../avgust-emanuel-reuss.md) - Avgust Emanuel Reuss (1811–1873) was a Czech-born geologist and paleontologist who served as a professor at the universities of Prague and Vienna and was a recognized expert on foraminifera, corals, and bryozoans.
* [Bernarda Žarn](../bernarda-zarn.md) - Slovenska televizijska voditeljica, rojena leta 1976 v Novem mestu, znana predvsem po dolgoletnem vodenju razvedrilnih oddaj na RTV Slovenija.
* [Dioniz Stur](../dioniz-stur.md) - Slovak-born geologist and paleontologist (1827–1893) who directed the Imperial Geological Institute in Vienna and produced foundational geological surveys of the Austrian Empire, including detailed mapping of present-day Slovenia.
* [Ester Sokler](../ester-sokler.md) - Ester Sokler is a Slovenian professional footballer who plays as a forward for Serbian club Radnički 1923 and has represented Slovenia at youth international levels.
* [Ferdinand Trenc](../ferdinand-trenc.md) - Ferdinand Trenc (1913–1976) was a Slovenian agricultural mechanization expert who promoted rational machine use and authored the book 'Siliranje krme'.
* [Franc Drenovec](../franc-drenovec.md) - Slovenski novinar in dopisnik (1917–1968), ki je kot dopisnik Tanjuga v Gani pomembno prispeval k poznavanju afriških osvobodilnih gibanj v Jugoslaviji.
* [Franc Trefalt](../franc-trefalt.md) - Slovenski amaterski in poklicni igralec (1903–1998), znan po naslovnih vlogah v Prešernovem gledališču v Kranju, kot so Hamlet, Tartuffe in Volpone.
* [Gabriel Giraud](../gabriel-giraud.md) - A French Trappist lay brother (1836–1899) who founded and funded the Rajhenburg Abbey in present-day Slovenia after the dissolution of his French monastery.
* [Gustav Johann Ludvik Tönnies](../gustav-johann-ludvik-tonnies.md) - A 19th-century building contractor and industrialist in Ljubljana who founded a major construction and iron foundry enterprise and built key railway, industrial, and public buildings across the Slovene lands.
* [Gustav Strniša](../gustav-strnisa.md) - Slovene poet and actor (1887–1970) known primarily for his children's poetry and prose, who also worked as a journalist and stage performer in Ljubljana.
* [Hinko Šuklje](../hinko-suklje.md) - Slovenski društveni delavec in ginekolog, rojen leta 1866 v Brestanici, ki je kot vodilni član celjskih narodnih društev in ustanovitelj tamburaškega kluba krepil narodno zavest na Štajerskem.
