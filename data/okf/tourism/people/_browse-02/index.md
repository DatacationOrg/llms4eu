# people

* [Ivan Matko](../ivan-matko.md) - Slovene internist (1885–1945) who served as primary physician in Maribor, founded the anti-tuberculosis league, and published extensively on internal medicine.
* [Ivan Vrščaj](../ivan-vrscaj.md) - Slovene educator and school supervisor active in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, known for his pedagogical work and leadership in Slovene minority schools across the Austro-Hungarian Littoral and Styria.
* [Ivan Škafar](../ivan-skafar.md) - Prekmurski zgodovinar in rimskokatoliški duhovnik, ki je raziskoval kulturno in cerkveno zgodovino Prekmurja ter prekmurske tiskane pesmarice.
* [Ivo I. Rudolf](../ivo-rudolf.md) - Slovenski zdravnik in avtor pesemskih besedil, rojen leta 1893 v Slovenskih Konjicah, katerega pesmi je uglasbila Breda Šček.
* [Jamšek, Franc](../jamsek-franc.md) - Slovenski šolnik, nadučitelj in šolski nadzornik, rojen leta 1840 v Žalcu, dejaven na Štajerskem v drugi polovici 19. stoletja.
* [Janez Krajnc](../janez-krajnc.md) - Slovene educator and school supervisor (1817–1908) who served as a model teacher under Bishop Slomšek and authored early Slovene primers.
* [Josip Brinar](../josip-brinar.md) - Slovene pedagogue, textbook author, and writer (1874–1959) known for introducing experimental pedagogy and for his youth literature and geographical name studies.
* [Josip Sivec](../josip-sivec.md) - Slovenian architect (1896–1974) known for early large-scale residential block construction in Ljubljana and industrial projects across Yugoslavia.
* [Josip Žičkar](../josip-zickar.md) - Slovenski politik in duhovnik (1846–1905), ki je kot deželni in državni poslanec na Spodnjem Štajerskem deloval za narodne pravice in ustanavljal katoliška podporna društva.
* [Jože Borštnar](../joze-borstnar.md) - Slovene military commander, politician, and railway manager (1915–1992) who served as political commissar of the 15th Division and commander of the 9th Corps during World War II, later becoming the first post-war commander of Ljubljana.
* [Jože Vidic](../joze-vidic.md) - Slovenski kronist narodnoosvobodilnega boja, častnik in publicist (1926–2018), znan po knjigah in člankih o vojnih dogodkih, žrtvah in vojnih zločincih.
* [Karel Erdlen](../karel-erdlen.md) - Slovenski veterinar (1923–1999), ki je deloval na območju Slovenske Bistrice in pomembno prispeval k izkoreninjanju živinskih kužnih bolezni ter uvajanju umetnega osemenjevanja.
* [Klemen Cehte](../klemen-cehte.md) - Klemen Cehte is a Slovenian handball player born in 1986 in Brežice, who plays as a left back for the Slovenian national team and the French club Pays d'Aix UCH.
* [Lojze Veberič](../lojze-veberic.md) - Lojze Veberič (born 1936) is a self-taught Slovenian painter and sculptor known for his portraits, landscapes, and experimental mixed-media sculptures in Prekmurje.
* [Maks Gatnik](../maks-gatnik.md) - Slovenski slikar amater in geodet, rojen leta 1910 v Brestanici, znan po akvarelih in delovanju v okviru Kluba slikarjev amaterjev 'Nikolaj Pirnat' v Novi Gorici.
* [Maksimilijan Držečnik](../maksimilijan-drzecnik.md) - Maksimilijan Držečnik (1903–1978) was a Slovenian Roman Catholic theologian, pastoral sociologist, and Bishop of Maribor who oversaw the first modern Slovenian translation of the Bible and was the only Slovenian bishop to attend all four sessions of the Second Vatican Council.
* [Marijan Tršar](../marijan-trsar.md) - Slovenski slikar, grafik, publicist in likovni kritik, rojen leta 1922 v Dolenjskih Toplicah, znan po svojem prehodu od realizma do neoekspresionistične sinteze ter po obsežnem kritiškem in pedagoškem delu.
* [Marjan Dovjak](../marjan-dovjak.md) - Marjan Dovjak (1928–1971) was a Slovenian painter known for his stylized, socially critical depictions of everyday life, evolving from color realism through post-cubist influences to a distinctive synthesis of expressive figurality.
* [Meri Avsenak Pogačnik](../meri-avsenak-pogacnik.md) - Slovenska pevka šansonov, glasbena urednica na Radiu Slovenija in dolgoletna sodelavka skladatelja Bojana Adamiča, rojena leta 1950.
* [Milan Grlj](../grlj-milan.md) - Slovene Catholic priest, folk poet, and national activist active in the Rijeka diocese and Podgraje, known for his oratory and rebuilding the Carmelite church after World War II.
