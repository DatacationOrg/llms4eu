Our educational programmes aim to foster additional interest in the cultural heritage of the [Rajhenburg Castle](../destinations/grad-rajhenburg), a cultural monument of national importance. The programmes are designed to correspond with school lesson plans of individual subjects (history, geography, Slovenian language and literature, arts education etc.) and optional classes (ethnology, etc.). Museum visits allow students to gain new knowledge about our cultural heritage or supplement their existing knowledge in an informal and relaxed manner. We offer programmes adapted for preschoolers, primary school pupils, and secondary school students. Our programmes can also be adapted in accordance with individual wishes.
