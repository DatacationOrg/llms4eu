**Experience the flavors in the restaurant at Rajhenburg Castle**

The cliff above the Sava River is adorned by Rajhenburg castle, which symbolizes our story created just for you. With respect for the history of the castle and the tradition of fish farming Pavlič, we accepted the challenge with a great deal of responsibility to create perfect culinary experiences with passion.

S spoštovanjem do grajske zgodovine in tradicije ribogojstva Pavlič smo z veliko mero odgovornosti sprejeli zaupan izziv, da s kulinaričnimi izkušnjami in strastjo ustvarimo popolna kulinarična doživetja.
