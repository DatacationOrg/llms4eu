# Peninoteka na Gradu Rajhenburg

Peninoteka na Gradu Rajhenburg is a sparkling wine tasting room situated within [Grad Rajhenburg](../destinations/grad-rajhenburg) in Krško, Slovenia.

## Tasting Information

Information regarding tasting sessions is available at the castle reception:

- **Phone:** 07 620 42 16
- **Mobile:** 051 390 648
- **Email:** [info@gradrajhenburg.si](mailto:info@gradrajhenburg.si)

## Additional Information

For more details, contact the Center za podjetništvo in turizem Krško:

- **Address:** Cesta krških žrtev 46, 8270 Krško
- **Phone:** 07 49 022 22
- **Mobile:** 041 353 505
- **Website:** [visitkrsko.com/vinske-kleti-in-vinotoci/peninoteka-na-gradu-rajhenburg](https://www.visitkrsko.com/vinske-kleti-in-vinotoci/peninoteka-na-gradu-rajhenburg)
- **Facebook:** [facebook.com/Visit-KR%C5%A0KO-495542747254370/](https://www.facebook.com/Visit-KR%C5%A0KO-495542747254370/)
