The Rajhenburg Castle history boasts the legacy of the Trappist monks, who were the first to carry out industrial chocolate production in Slovenia.

Today chocolate is produced at the House of Mozer near [Grad Rajhenburg](../destinations/grad-rajhenburg) in collaboration with the Kunej House of Vines, Wine, and Chocolate, Kunej Aleš s.p. This is a lasting effect of the ‘The influence of the Trappist order on the Posavje countryside’ project, acronym Trapistin.

## Products and Purchase

Excellent chocolate, chocolate wine, and other chocolate products by the producer **Kunej vino & čokolada d.o.o.** can be purchased in the [museum shop at Rajhenburg Castle](../services/museum-shop-rajhenburg).

## Tastings and Workshops

For pre-announced groups, a tasting of chocolate products or a workshop where you can prepare your own chocolate is organized.

Information about the workshop is available at the Rajhenburg Castle reception:

- T: 07 620 42 16
- M: 051 390 648
- E: [info@gradrajhenburg.si](mailto:info@gradrajhenburg.si)

## Producer Contact

**Kunej vino & čokolada d.o.o.**

Cesta prvih borcev 40, 8280 Brestanica, DŠ: SI 52525813

- T: 07 49 73 330
- M: 031 337 526
