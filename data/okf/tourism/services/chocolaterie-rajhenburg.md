The Rajhenburg Castle history boasts the legacy of the Trappist monks, who were the first to carry out industrial chocolate production in Slovenia.

Today chocolate is produced at the House of Mozer near Rajhenburg Castle in collaboration with the Kunej House of Vines, Wine, and Chocolate, Kunej Aleš s.p. This is a lasting effect of the ‘The influence of the Trappist order on the Posavje countryside’ project, acronym Trapistin.

Excellent chocolate, chocolate wine, and other chocolate products by the producer **Kunej vino & čokolada d.o.o.** can be purchased in the museum shop of Rajhenburg Castle.

For pre-announced groups, a tasting of chocolate products or a workshop where you can prepare your own chocolate is organized.
