The Rajhenburg Castle's museum shop boasts a distinctive and interesting architecture. Visitors can choose from among hand-crafted artisan products by Posavje craftsmen, modern artistic products, as well as museum-related materials and publications; the chocolate treats are, naturally, among the most desired. Hand-crafted products and gifts on offer reflect a combination of traditional skill and knowledge rooted in our heritage, but are simultaneously born from modern thought and creativity extending beyond the limits of our immediate cultural environment.

The selection of products on offer is thematically linked to our permanent and temporary exhibitions as well as stories relating to the Rajhenburg Castle, its visitors, and its surrounding area.

Muzejska trgovina Gradu Rajhenburg je z umestitvijo v grajske prostore in izbrano ponudbo svojevrstna izkušnja in srečanje z zgodovino in dediščino, z vsakdanjim in prazničnim načinom življenja.

Pri izboru izdelkov sledimo, da so ti tematsko povezani s stalnimi in občasnimi razstavami in z zgodbami, povezanimi z gradom samim, njegovimi nekdanjimi lastniki in okolico.

Na voljo so vam izbrani rokodelski izdelki posavskih rokodelcev, izdelki sodobne ustvarjalnosti ter muzejska gradiva in publikacije, ki skupaj predstavljajo preplet tradicionalnih veščin in znanj naše dediščine, so plod sodobnega razmišljanja in ustvarjalnosti ter rezultat strokovnega dela muzejskih sodelavcev.
