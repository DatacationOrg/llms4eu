**Visit the castles and monasteries of Posavje ... ... and discover their rich heritage; spend a night, have fun, and enjoy the experience of centuries recounted by the walls and people**

The landscape between rivers Mirna, Sava, Krka and Sotla is marked by castles. They have been forming part of this cultural environment for more than a millennium, bearing witness to local life, and the influence of those who passed through, be it due East or West.

No matter from which cardinal direction or by which path you arrive, when you enter the region of Posavje, you will be welcomed by a castle. If you start your journey in Sevnica, from the castle situated on a hill above the Sava River, you can follow the river past [Grad Rajhenburg](destinations/grad-rajhenburg) all the way to the Brežice Castle. There, at the confluence of rivers Sava and Krka you can turn back to follow Krka towards Kostanjevica na Krki and its once mighty monastery, or continue along Sava to Mokrice Castle. No matter which direction you are coming from, you will be able to reach Podsreda Castle, which connects Posavje to Kozjansko.

In the near future, we hope to extend the trek to include castles Raka, Šrajbarski turn (Leskovec pri Krškem), Bizeljsko, and Pišece. However, for now these have either yet to be restored or remain inaccessible to the general public.

The leaflet we have prepared to entice you to visit the castles and the monastery in Kostanjevica also includes basic information about these structures. We hope our sweepstakes and the prize awaiting you when you finish your journey through Posavje - the land of castles, will further tempt you to visit.

Above all, we aim to draw you to journey through the centuries by interweaving the past and the present in our events; we hope you come back and visit our castles again, not just for our museum collections, but for the events, concerts, performances, excellence on offer, enjoyment, and the knowledge that you are always welcome here.

Alenka Černelič Krošelj, curator, director of Posavski Museum Brežice

## The Castles of Posavje Project

Castles co-create our cultural landscape and our identity. Especially in Slovenia, where we boast numerous excellently preserved and restored castles, as well as castles that constantly remind us that more needs to be done.

In Posavje, where there are several castles with different contents, owners, and managers in a small area, we have joined together in an informal association, [Gradovi Posavja](https://gradoviposavja.si/), because we want to connect and upgrade our work.

The project, which we named **Gradovi Posavja** (Castles of Posavje), was formed in 2014 and is sustainability-oriented. It includes the managers of six castles in Posavje who have joined the informal association:

- **Galerija Božidar Jakac Kostanjevica na Krki**, which manages the former monastery in Kostanjevica na Krki
- **Kozjanski park Podsreda**, which manages **Grad Podsreda**
- **Kulturni dom Krško**, **enota Grad Rajhenburg**, which manages **Grad Rajhenburg**
- **KŠTM Sevnica**, which manages **Grad Sevnica**
- **Posavski muzej Brežice**, which manages **Grad Brežice**
- **Terme Čatež**, which owns **Grad Mokrice**

The **Ministrstvo za kulturo** (Ministry of Culture), as the owner of **Grad Šrajbarski turn**, **Grad Bizeljsko**, and **Grad Pišece**, was also invited to the group, but due to the inaccessibility or not-yet-opened status of the castles, they decided not to participate, although we keep them informed about everything.

As the first "external" promotional step, a **brochure Gradovi Posavja** was issued at the end of 2015, with the collaboration of 5 managers. It was published in December 2015, with 16 pages, in two languages (Slovenian and English), in 30,000 copies.

During the design and preparation of the joint brochure, we prepared a joint project proposal that includes various connections, especially in the field of programs and promotion, with the aim of co-creating a strong and connecting **"cultural and tourist unit" Gradovi Posavja**.

United under the regional name Gradovi Posavja, we follow the outlined project to the best of our ability, which includes **program and promotional-marketing connections** of castles in Posavje, establishes a new brand with the aim of joint promotion of public and market services, and commits to finding ways for cooperation and connection also outside Posavje. The goal of the project is to **raise the visibility** and **added value of the castle network** with an innovative approach, as well as all existing and developing activities in the region, and thereby achieve the greatest possible opening to the wider Slovenian and international space.
