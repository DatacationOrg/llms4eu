Nedaleč od Krškega, v smeri proti Celju, leži ob sotočju reke Save in potoka Brestanica kraj Brestanica, nekoč imenovan *Rajhenburg*.

Brestanica je urbano naselje mestnega značaja in je sedež krajevne skupnosti.

Površina KS Brestanica je **1,9 km 2**, nadmorska višina je

**177,9 m**, število prebivalcev je

**973**(popis leta 2002).

Na naključnega popotnika Brestanica takoj ne naredi močnejšega vtisa, saj poteka pot v kraj skozi ozko dolino, ki pa se ob koncu trškega jedra počasi razširi v prijeten pogled na celoto.

This source part provides a poetic overview of Brestanica's geography, describing its location at the confluence of the Sava and Brestanica stream, dominated by Rajhenburg Castle. The text emphasizes the castle's history with the Lords of Rajhenburg and Trappist monks, the Bazilika Marije Lurške, and the panoramic view from Mohorjeva cerkev on Sremič.

- Rajhenburg Castle dominates the confluence of the Sava River and the Brestanica stream.
- Brestanica was first mentioned in written sources in 895.
- The settlement was formerly named Rajhenburg.
- The former settlement of Rajhenburg was squeezed into a gorge between the castle hill and the wine-growing Sremič hill.
- After the war, the settlement was renamed Brestanica after the Brestanica stream.
- Brestanica expanded northwards into a basin reaching the foothills of Bohor.
- The green slopes around Brestanica are dotted with small churches and hamlets.
- Rajhenburg Castle tells stories of its lords, the particularly well-known Lords of Rajhenburg.
- Rajhenburg Castle reveals the secret life of the Trappist monks who managed it for almost 60 years.

- The Bazilika Marije Lurške is located in the basin of Brestanica.
