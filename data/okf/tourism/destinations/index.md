# destinations

* [Bazilika Lurške Marije, Brestanica](bazilika-lurske-marije-brestanica.md) - A monumental three-nave neo-Romanesque minor basilica and parish church in Brestanica, Slovenia, built between 1908 and 1914, dedicated to Our Lady of Lourdes, and known as the 'Slovenian Lourdes'.
* [Brestanica](brestanica.md) - A settlement in Slovenia near Krško, historically known as Rajhenburg, situated at the confluence of the Sava River and Brestanica stream, dominated by Rajhenburg Castle, and first mentioned in 895.
* [Celeja](celeia.md) - Celeja (Municipium Claudium Celeia) was a Roman city located in the area of present-day Celje, Slovenia, that flourished as a major colony and crossroads from the 1st century BC to the 6th century AD.
* [Cerkev sv. Boštjana, Brestanica](cerkev-sv-bostjana-brestanica.md) - A church in Brestanica, Slovenia, built on a hill at the confluence of the Brestanica and Senovo streams, whose last thorough renovation in 1996 disproved assumptions about its construction in 1523.
* [Cerkev sv. Mohorja nad Brestanico](cerkev-sv-mohorja-nad-brestanico.md) - A single-nave church above Brestanica, Slovenia, with a likely Romanesque core and a notable Notburga chapel built in 1744 and richly frescoed in 1750.
* [Cerkev sv. Petra, Brestanica](cerkev-sv-petra-brestanica.md) - A late Gothic church with Romanesque origins and Baroque additions located in the oldest part of Brestanica, Slovenia, featuring a 1592 black marble main altar and Renaissance tombstones.
* [Gostišče Ribnik](gostisce-ribnik.md) - A guesthouse located in a forest by ponds in Brestanica, Slovenia, offering a peaceful setting for relaxation.
* [Mestna občina Krško](mestna-obcina-krsko.md) - Mestna občina Krško is a municipality in southeastern Slovenia with around 26,000 inhabitants, known for housing the country's only nuclear power plant.
* [Neviodunum](neviodunum.md) - Neviodunum was a Roman settlement and later municipium located in present-day Drnovo, Slovenia, which served as an important port and trading center on the route between Emona and Siscia.
* [Posavska statistična regija](posavska-statisticna-regija.md) - A statistical region in eastern Slovenia covering 968 km² across six municipalities, known for the Krško Nuclear Power Plant and the Čatež thermal spa.
* [Rajhenburg Castle](rajhenburg-castle.md) - A medieval castle in Brestanica, Slovenia, built in the 12th century, later used as a Trappist monastery, Nazi deportation camp, and women's prison, now serving as a museum and cultural venue.
* [Senovo](senovo.md) - Senovo je naselje v Občini Krško na Štajerskem, ki se je razvilo v rudarsko središče po prvi svetovni vojni zaradi kopanja rjavega premoga.
* [Sevnica](sevnica.md) - Sevnica is a medium-sized town and the administrative center of the Municipality of Sevnica in Slovenia, located on the left bank of the Sava River and historically known as Lichtenwald.
* [Slovenija](slovenia.md) - Slovenija je srednjeevropska država na stičišču Alp, Sredozemlja, Panonske nižine in Dinarskega gorstva, ki meji na Italijo, Avstrijo, Madžarsko in Hrvaško ter ima izhod na Jadransko morje.
* [The Castles of Posavje](castles-of-posavje.md) - A cultural tourism route in the Posavje region of Slovenia linking restored medieval castles and a monastery along the Sava and Krka rivers.
* [Trapistovski samostan Marije Rešiteljice v Rajhenburgu](trapist-monastery-rajhenburg.md) - A former Trappist monastery in Rajhenburg Castle, where monks lived under strict rules from 1881 until its dissolution in 1947, engaging in agriculture and, from 1896, industrial production of chocolate and liqueurs.
* [Štajerska](stajerska.md) - Štajerska, also known as Spodnja Štajerska, is a historical region in northeastern Slovenia covering 6,050 km², with Maribor as its economic center.
