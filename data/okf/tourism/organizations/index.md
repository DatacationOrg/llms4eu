# organizations

* [Društvo Bohorska konjenica](drustvo-bohorska-konjenica.md) - A Slovenian equestrian association based in Dolenji Leskovec, Brestanica, represented by Božo Abram.
* [Društvo prijateljev mladine Brestanica](drustvo-prijateljev-mladine-brestanica.md) - A voluntary, non-profit association based in Brestanica, Slovenia, dedicated to improving the quality of leisure time for children, adolescents, parents, and families through organized events and activities.
* [Društvo upokojencev Brestanica](drustvo-upokojencev-brestanica.md) - A pensioners' association in Brestanica, Slovenia, founded in 1947, which provides social, recreational, and volunteer activities for its 338 members.
* [Gorniki Brestanica](gorniki-brestanica.md) - A group of mountain guides from Brestanica, Slovenia, active since 2005 as part of the Bohor Senovo mountaineering society.
* [Krajevna skupnost Brestanica](krajevna-skupnost-brestanica.md) - Local community administrative office for Brestanica, Slovenia, located at Cesta prvih borcev 12.
* [Kulturni dom Krško](kulturni-dom-krsko.md) - A Slovenian public cultural institution based in Krško that manages Castle Rajhenburg, Krško City Museum, and Krško Gallery.
* [Kulturno društvo Svoboda Brestanica](kulturno-drustvo-svoboda-brestanica.md) - A Slovenian amateur cultural society based in Brestanica that operates choral, theatre, dance, art, and literary sections along with a community library.
* [Odbojkarsko društvo Brestanica](odbojkarsko-drustvo-brestanica.md) - A volleyball club based in Brestanica, Slovenia, responsible for developing volleyball in the Posavje region with youth and recreational teams.
* [Prostovoljno gasilsko društvo Brestanica](prostovoljno-gasilsko-drustvo-brestanica.md) - A volunteer fire department founded in 1893, operating in Brestanica and surrounding settlements within the Krško Firefighting Association.
* [Statistični urad Republike Slovenije](statisticni-urad-republike-slovenije.md) - The Statistical Office of the Republic of Slovenia (SURS) is the Slovenian government service responsible for conducting national statistics, established in its current form in 1995.
* [Svet KS Brestanica](svet-ks-brestanica.md) - The local community council of Brestanica, Slovenia, led by president Vlado Bezjak.
* [Teniški klub Brestanica](teniski-klub-brestanica.md) - A tennis club in Brestanica, Slovenia, offering recreational tennis practice, a tennis school, and tournament organization.
* [Turistično društvo Brestanica](turisticno-drustvo-brestanica.md) - A local tourism and cultural association in Brestanica, Slovenia, founded in 1964, known for organizing community events and contributing to the restoration of Rajhenburg Castle.
* [Združenje borcev za vrednote NOB Krško](zdruzenje-borcev-za-vrednote-nob-krsko.md) - A local branch of the Slovenian veterans' association in Brestanica that promotes the values of the National Liberation Struggle and participates in commemorative events.
