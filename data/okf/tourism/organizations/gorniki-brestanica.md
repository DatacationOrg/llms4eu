## About

Gorniki v Brestanici smo le fantje, ki radi zahajamo v gore tako poleti kot pozimi.

V letu 2005 smo uspešno opravili tečaj za vodnike PZS.

Včlanjeni smo v [PD Bohor Senovo](../organizations/bohorska-konjenica), kjer v prostem času delujemo kot vodniki na organiziranih pohodih.

## Gorniki Brestanica – Brestanica

A webpage on brestanica.com introducing Gorniki Brestanica, a group of male mountaineering guides from Brestanica, Slovenia. It provides their website, email contact, states they enjoy mountains in summer and winter, notes they completed the Alpine Association of Slovenia (PZS) guide course in 2005, and mentions their membership and volunteer guiding activities within the Bohor Senovo Mountaineering Society (PD Bohor Senovo).
