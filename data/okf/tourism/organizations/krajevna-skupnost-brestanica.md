## Krajevna skupnost – Brestanica

Contact and office hours page for the Krajevna skupnost Brestanica, listing the address, phone, fax, email, and the secretary Aleksandra Brodnik with her direct contact details.

- The fax number for Krajevna skupnost Brestanica is 07 497 16 38.
