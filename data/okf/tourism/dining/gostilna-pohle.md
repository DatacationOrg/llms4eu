**Gostilna Pohle** (also known as **Pohle Inn** or **Gostilna s prenočišči Pohle**) is a family-run inn located at Cesta na ribnik 3a, 8280 [Brestanica](../destinations/brestanica).

## Contact

- **Tel.:** 07 49 73 065
- **Fax:** 07 49 73 066
- **Tennis reservation:** 07 49 71 112
- **e-mail:** pohle.m@siol.net

## Facilities & Capacities

### Accommodation
- 8 double rooms with private bathroom (TWC), cable TV, and balcony

### Dining
- Restaurant with 130 seats, offering classic cuisine
- Terrace with 35 seats

### Recreation
- 2 artificial grass tennis courts
- Seasonal outdoor swimming pool
- Parking
