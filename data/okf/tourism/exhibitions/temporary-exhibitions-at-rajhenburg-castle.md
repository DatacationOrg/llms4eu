# Temporary Exhibitions at Rajhenburg Castle

Rajhenburg Castle in Brestanica, Slovenia, hosts a dynamic program of temporary exhibitions, primarily focusing on contemporary visual arts. These exhibitions are presented within the historical architecture of the castle, often engaging with its unique spaces and complex history.

## Current and Upcoming Exhibitions

### Simona Šuc, Pokrajina razdalje (Landscape of Distance)
- **Duration:** 27. March 2026 – 28. February 2027
- **Description:** Painter Simona Šuc presents works whose common denominator is drawing. The line, as the basic building block of compositions, travels freely across the painting surface, forming circular or crystalline shapes, firmly connected or interrupted. With varying rhythm and form, it composes dynamic landscapes. The artist transfers the question of distance into communication with the viewer, as the dynamic installation and unconventional approaches to the painting medium break the established frameworks of presenting paintings and place them in close contact with the viewer. The viewer enters a landscape that evokes joy and wonder, the kind that has always accompanied explorers in their discoveries.

### Hana Stupica, Med svetlobo in sencami (Between Light and Shadows)
- **Duration:** 25. October 2025 – 7. December 2025
- **Description:** Hana Stupica's exhibition in the tower of Rajhenburg Castle features her illustrations for Schönwerth's fairy tales, *Zajčkova hišica* (The Bunny's House), and *Zgodbe za vina Fedora* (Stories for Fedor's Wines). Illustration is inseparably linked to the story it refers to, but the meaning of 'picture in a book' only developed in the 19th century; the term itself means to illustrate, derived from the Latin for light – to illuminate. The artist's illustrations come to life in their own independent meaning, regardless of whether we know the works for which they were created.

### Zven kamna (The Sound of Stone)
- **Duration:** 4. September 2025 – 12. October 2025
- **Description:** A musical-sculptural project created through the collaboration of the Slovenian Percussion Project (Slovenski tolkalni projekt) and academic sculptor Alenka Vidrgar. Stone is not only an aesthetic or visual element, nor merely a source of sound, but a visual-acoustic synergy. The sculptor and percussionists spent considerable time in quarries and the studio, researching and seeking the sound of stone. The results are inspiring. *Zven kamna* is an innovative project reflecting the mutual complementarity of musical and sculptural art. It is also interactive; mallets are available next to the sculptures so visitors can create and hear the stone themselves. The sculptures are made from selected stone with excellent acoustic properties from the Lipica quarry. The project is a continuation of the *Zveneče skulpture* (Sounding Sculptures) project, performed at the Ljubljana Festival 2016. Since then, the exhibition and concerts have toured domestically and abroad (Maribor, Koper, Celovec/Klagenfurt, Milje/Muggia, etc.). Text: Franci Krevh, Alenka Vidrgar.

### Oto Rimele, Koprnenja svetlobe (Yearnings of Light)
- **Duration:** 17. April 2025 – 8. March 2026
- **Description:** Oto Rimele is a deep explorer of light, known for his paintings-objects which he calls "light catchers." His refined painting process achieves an impression of complete dematerialisation of the artefacts. The visitor is overwhelmed by the tactility of relief-like coloured shadows glowing like an aura around the object. Rimele exposes his creations to daylight whenever possible, making his exhibitions a remarkable experience of luminous variability that changes at every moment. His paintings demand a contemplative approach, inviting viewers to go deep inside themselves and, in the artist's words, "move from the anthropocentric position of the contemporary times into the zone of natural and cosmic order."

### Janko Orač, Grafika osebno (Printmaking Personally)
- **Duration:** 4. April 2025 – 1. March 2026
- **Description:** This exhibition showcases the artistic maturity and sovereignty of Janko Orač, evident in his work from the last decade. Orač's practice is split between painting and printmaking, constructing works with a signature rhythmic layering of colours and applications of paint, and the arrangement of signs and symbols. Most of the presented works date from the last two to three years, building on his print cycles *Nocturno* and *Odtisi globin* (Prints of Depths).

## Past Exhibitions

### Martina Koritnik Fajt, Retrospektiva (Retrospective)
- **Duration:** 1. March 2024 – 2. March 2025
- **Description:** A retrospective of Martina Koritnik Fajt (1947−2020), who graduated in 1973 from the Academy of Fine Arts and Design in Ljubljana in the class of Professor Gabrijel Stupica. The most noticeable influence on her artistic poetics was Professor Zoran Didek. She was a prominent cultural and educational figure in Kostanjevica na Krki and Podbočje, and lived in Krško. From 1993, she worked as a freelance artist.

### Andrej Brumen Čop I Posebno osebno: podobe oseb (I Personally Personable: Pictures of People)
- **Duration:** 17. February 2023 – 30. December 2023
- **Description:** Andrej Brumen Čop focuses on figurative motifs using drawing, watercolour, painting, and less often, printmaking. This exhibition featured paintings of the human figure, including portraits, nudes, and self-portraits. A significant group of paintings depicted people involved in current or past events, where the sitter is caught in a moment that tells a story.

### Boštjan Kavčič I PREHAJANJA. (Transitions: Sculptures from the Point of Perception)
- **Duration:** 21. May 2022 – 30. September 2023
- **Description:** Boštjan Kavčič, a middle-generation Slovenian artist, presented sculptures guided by mastery of matter and intuition. He consciously uses reusable natural materials, preferring stone. His series *ORGanisms*, begun in 2002, comprises over eighty stone sculptures. The exhibition's starting point was the history of the castle complex, including the Trappist Order's settlement in 1881 and the castle's later use as a Nazi exile camp and a post-war penal institution for political prisoners. Kavčič works on the stone's surface by drawing spiral notches to restore the flow of energy he believes has come to a standstill.

### Irena Romih I VAJE IZ OPTIMIZMA (EXERCISES IN OPTIMISM)
- **Duration:** 22. April 2022 – 30. December 2022
- **Description:** The exhibition was extended until 30 December 2022.

### Mateja Kavčič I DREVO (TREE)
- **Duration:** 25. September 2021 – 5. December 2021
- **Description:** In the attic of the round tower of Rajhenburg Castle, an exhibition *Drevo* (Tree) by artist Mateja Kavčič was opened. Curator Nina Jeza, Artists&Poor s, wrote in the catalogue: "Mateja Kavčič presents her personal 'artist's tree' in the form of an impressive environmental installation made from natural materials, which she placed in the magnificent timber framing of the attic of the round tower of Rajhenburg Castle, thus intertwining cultural heritage with contemporary art."

### Iva Tratnik | IGRAČE IN KOSTI (TOYS AND BONES)
- **Duration:** 17. September 2021 – 13. February 2022
- **Description:** Iva Tratnik's exhibition was divided into two parts. The main part, showcasing paintings and textile collages, was installed in the Great Hall. The second part, a spatial-sound installation, was featured separately in the Round Hall. All works were created in the last 10 years, with a focus on the most recent ones.

### Vladimir Leben / Making Ends Meet
- **Duration:** 9. October 2020 – 15. August 2021
- **Description:** Vladimir Leben presented a series of wooden objects and large-scale oil paintings on canvas featuring animals as principal protagonists. The animals perform "human" acts in intensely coloured, narrative scenes that reflect a view of contemporary Anthropocene society. Leben is one of the founders of The Museum of Too-Modern Art, a collective that rejected academic elitism.

### Contemporary Artists from Trieste
- **Duration:** 19. October 2019 – 19. April 2020
- **Description:** A miscellany of exhibitions featuring artists from Trieste.

### The American Dream
- **Duration:** 28. June 2019 – 15. September 2019

### If Slovenia were
- **Duration:** 16. November 2018 – 18. May 2019
- **Description:** A group exhibition curated by Klavdij Sluban, featuring works by Jošt Dolinšek, Jošt Franko, Katja Goljat, Ciril Jazbec, Irena Jurca, Jurij Korenjak, Primož Korošec, Tereza Kozinc, Meta Krese, Robert Marin, Dejan Mijović, Matej Povše, Boštjan Pucelj, Matjaž Rušt, Klemen Skubic, Nina Sotelšek, Ana Zibelnik, and Manja Zore.
